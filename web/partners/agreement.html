ZIP Sponsorship Agreement

Version 1.0 · Effective August 6, 2026 · Between Yayday LLC, doing business as ShouldISellYet.com ("we," "us"), and the licensed real estate professional who accepts it ("you," "Sponsor").

1. What you are buying

Advertising placement, and nothing else. For a flat fee of $39 per month per ZIP code, we display your name, license details, and contact information alongside the market page for that ZIP code. You are buying ad space on a website. You are not buying leads, introductions, referrals, endorsements, or any representation about how many people will see or respond to your placement.

2. What "exclusive" means, and what it does not

A sponsored ZIP code carries one sponsor at a time. That exclusivity is exclusive advertising placement in that ZIP code — no other sponsor's advertisement appears there while yours does.

It is not exclusive representation of any consumer, not a territory, and not any claim on the business of people who live in that ZIP code. Homeowners are free to contact anyone. Nothing here creates any relationship between you and any consumer; any such relationship is formed by you and them directly, on your own terms, and we are not a party to it.

3. No compensation tied to transactions

The fee is flat and fixed. No per-transaction compensation of any kind flows in either direction — no commission, no commission split, no referral fee, no success fee, no bonus, and no payment contingent on whether any consumer contacts you, lists with you, buys through you, or closes anything. We are paid the same whether your placement produces a hundred transactions or none.

4. No brokerage relationship

Yayday LLC is not a real estate brokerage and holds no real estate license. Nothing in this agreement creates a brokerage, agency, partnership, joint venture, employment, or franchise relationship between you and us. You are an independent business. We do not supervise your work, and you do not act for us.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Your own advertising compliance. Real estate advertising is regulated, and the rules differ by state and by brokerage. You are solely responsible for ensuring your placement complies with them — including license display, brokerage identification, fair housing requirements, and any disclosure your jurisdiction requires.
  • Your broker's approval. If your license is held with a brokerage, you are responsible for obtaining whatever approval your broker requires before advertising here, and for keeping it.
  • Accuracy. Everything you give us to display — name, license number, brokerage, contact details, biography — must be accurate and current, and you must tell us when it changes.
  • A valid license. You must hold an active real estate license in the state covering the ZIP code you sponsor, for the whole time you sponsor it.

6. Verdicts are never for sale

Sponsorship buys placement and has no influence on any verdict, signal, threshold, or ranking. Verdicts are computed from public market data by fixed, published thresholds, before we know who sponsors any ZIP code. We will not alter, withhold, or soften a verdict for a sponsor, and we will not represent to any consumer that a sponsor is endorsed or recommended by us. If this is a problem for you, do not sponsor.

7. Term, billing, and cancellation

  • Month to month. Billing recurs monthly through Stripe until cancelled.
  • You may cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period you have already paid for; your placement runs until then.
  • Fees already paid are not refunded or prorated.
  • We may change the fee with at least 30 days' notice before it applies to you.

8. We may decline or end a sponsorship

We may decline an application, or end an active sponsorship, at our discretion — including where we cannot verify a license, where a license lapses or is disciplined, where conduct toward consumers is misrepresentative or unprofessional, or where continuing would put us in conflict with a source's terms or a regulator. If we end a sponsorship for any reason other than your breach, we refund the unused portion of the period you have paid for.

9. The service is provided "as is"

Placement is provided as is and as available. We do not warrant uptime, traffic, visibility, or results of any kind. To the maximum extent permitted by law, our total liability for all claims related to this agreement is limited to the sponsorship fees you paid us in the twelve months before the claim arose, and we are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, or for lost profits or lost opportunity.

We may update this agreement; material changes will be posted here with a new version number and effective date, and will apply to you from your next billing period. Questions: support@shouldisellyet.com. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Maryland, USA.
