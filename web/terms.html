7. Data sources, what they measure, and how often they refresh

Readings are computed from a licensed third-party market-statistics API. Supporting context uses U.S. Government sources in the public domain (the Federal Housing Finance Agency house price index and U.S. Census Bureau geography files) and Freddie Mac's mortgage-rate survey. We do not own the underlying third-party data and grant you no rights in it.

The measurement basis changed in August 2026. Readings were previously computed largely from statistics about homes that had sold. They are now computed from statistics about homes currently listed for sale. Asking prices run higher than sale prices, and time on market measured across unsold listings runs longer than time to contract measured across homes that sold. A figure shown today is a different kind of number than one shown before that date, even where the reading word is the same.

Freshness varies by ZIP code. Market data is licensed per ZIP code, so busier ZIP codes are refreshed more often than the rest, and two readings you see may be of different ages. We do not represent that every ZIP code is equally current. Where a page shows a reading, it also shows the date its own data runs through. The current cadence is stated on the methodology page.
