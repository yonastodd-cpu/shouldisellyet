What we crunch

Four signals that move months before prices do — with the exact danger lines we use. We test the same signals in both directions — so you hear it early whether your market is cracking or heating up.
SIGNAL 01
Months of supply

How long it would take to sell every home on the market. Above 4 months, sellers lose pricing power. The single best early signal.

Danger line: 4.0 mo
SIGNAL 02
Price trend

The typical sale price versus a year ago — cross-checked against price per square foot so condo-mix distortions don't fool you.

Danger line: −2% y/y

Time to sell

Homes sitting longer is the first visible crack, appearing months before prices actually move.

Danger line: +40% y/y

Price cuts & supply waves

When over a third of listings cut asking price — or inventory surges — competition is building for the day you sell.
